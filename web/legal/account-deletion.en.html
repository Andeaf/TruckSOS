TruckSOS — Delete account

The single page: https://trucksos.net/legal/account-deletion (the old address trucksos.net/account-deletion redirects here). Operator: Breană David Eduard, natural person, Romania · support@trucksos.net

From the app (recommended)

Profile → Settings → Delete account → confirm with your password (or with the confirmation word, if you signed in with Google/Apple). Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

If you no longer have the app installed

Send an e-mail from the account’s address to support@trucksos.net with the subject “Ștergere cont” (“Delete account”). We will confirm your identity (reply from the same address) and delete the account within 7 days at the latest; you will receive confirmation by e-mail.

What is deleted immediately

The sign-in account (e-mail, password, Google/Apple sign-ins), the profile (name, photo, telephone number, address, VAT number, contact e-mail, position), trucks, breakdown announcements and their photos, applications, Market listings and their photos, ratings you gave, favourites, blocks, search history, preferences and notification tokens, moderation notices, the connection diagnostic log, messages not yet sent from the queue, your photos and files in chat (except those that are evidence in a report still open — they are deleted when it is closed), the Apple token (after we revoke the authorisation at Apple, if you signed in with Apple).

What is kept (pseudonymised, without name and without contact details)

  • Text messages you sent in other people’s private conversations and in the community channel remain visible, marked “deleted user” — so that others keep their conversations; they can no longer be linked to you.
  • Ratings received by mechanics remain in their average (the author appears as “deleted user”).
  • The log of moderation actions and reports (technical account identifier, date, decision) — 12 months, legal obligation (DSA); no personal data in clear text.
  • The record of acceptance of the terms (technical identifier, version, date) — 3 years.
  • The technical record of the deletion (identifier, date, whether the Apple authorisation was revoked) — 12 months.
  • The technical error reports you sent from the “Something went wrong” screen — without directly identifying data (only a short code derived from your account; no e-mail, phone or message content) — are kept for 30 days and are then deleted automatically.

Data at other providers

Notifications already delivered remain on your device; account e-mails sent earlier remain in your inbox. Our providers (the list is in the Privacy policy) delete technical copies under their own periods (generally under 30 days).

Export before deletion

From Settings → Download my data you receive a JSON file with your account data and the content you created (twice a day); the file does not include the technical logs — the connection diagnostic log and the technical error reports. We recommend that you download it before deleting the account: after deletion we can no longer recover anything.
