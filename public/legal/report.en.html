TruckSOS — Reports (illegal content or content that breaches the terms)

Public page: https://trucksos.net/report · can also be used without an account · Operator: Breană David Eduard, natural person, Romania · abuse@trucksos.net

Page text

If you have seen in TruckSOS an announcement, a message, a profile or a rating that breaks the law or our Terms, tell us here. You do not need an account. You can also report by e-mail to abuse@trucksos.net.

What happens after you submit: you receive a confirmation at the e-mail address you give; we analyse the report and decide within 48 hours if it concerns manifestly illegal content, respectively within 7 days in other cases; we tell you the decision by e-mail. The author of the content receives the reasons and can contest within 14 days. Reports submitted in bad faith may be disregarded.

What to include: the link or identifier of the content (for example a profile link; if you have neither, describe the content — who published it, where and when you saw it — in an e-mail to abuse@trucksos.net), the reason, a short explanation and, where relevant, why you consider the content illegal (the law breached). If the report concerns your own rights (for example photos of you or your trademark), tell us, and in what capacity you are making it.

Your data: your e-mail address and the content of the report are used only to handle it and to reply to you; they are kept for 12 months after closure (see the Privacy policy). We do not share them with the author of the content, except where the law requires it.

The form (labels)

  • Type of content: Breakdown announcement / Market listing / Channel message / Private message (only if you are a participant) / User / Rating
  • Identifier or link: (precompletat din aplicație)
  • Reason: Spam or fraud · Illegal content · Harassment or threats · Personal data of someone else · Sexual or violent content · Infringes my rights (photos, trademark) · Other
  • Explanation (optional, max 2000 characters)
  • Your e-mail address (required, for confirmation and reply)
  • ☐ I declare in good faith that the information in this report is correct and complete. (required — DSA Art. 16(2)(d))
  • Anti-bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile)
  • Button: Submit report

Messages

  • Success: “Thank you. We have received your report; you will receive a confirmation and the decision at {{email}}.”
  • Verification error: “The anti-bot check failed. Please try again.”
  • Too many submissions: “You have sent too many reports within an hour. Try again later or write to us at abuse@trucksos.net.”
  • No e-mail: “We need an e-mail address so that we can reply to you.”
