TruckSOS — Privacy policy

Version 1.1.0 · In force from the release date of version 1.1.0 of the app · Replaces the version of 1 November 2026.

1. Who we are (the operator)

TruckSOS is a mobile and web application that connects truck drivers whose vehicles have broken down with available mechanics. It also includes a section for sale listings (Market) between users and a public community discussion channel.

The controller of personal data is Breană David Eduard, natural person, Romania, e-mail support@trucksos.net. We have not appointed a data protection officer; any request about your data should be sent to the address above.

2. What data we process

  • Account data: e-mail address and password (the password is stored encrypted by the authentication provider, Supabase; we never see it). When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive only your e-mail address (or the Apple relay address, if you choose “Hide My Email”) and your name; we do not receive your Google or Apple account password. If you sign in with Apple from the iOS app, we keep an encrypted technical token issued by Apple, used exclusively to revoke the authorisation at Apple when you delete your account (an Apple requirement); we do not use it for any other purpose.
  • Profile data: name, role (driver / mechanic), profile photo, language; for mechanics: specialisation, area of activity, company, address, VAT number, contact e-mail; telephone number (both roles); approximate GPS position, if you set one for distances.
  • Reports without an account (the trucksos.net/report page): the e-mail address you provide and the content of the report, used only to handle it and reply to you (see §7); no account is needed.
  • Content: breakdown announcements (description, location, coordinates, truck make and model, registration number and chassis number — data you provide yourself), saved trucks, mechanics’ applications (optional price offer, message), Market listings (title, description, price, photos), private and channel messages (text, photos, videos with their sound — recorded from the conversation, in which case the microphone is used only while you are recording, or picked from your gallery —, files, including audio files), ratings, favourites, blocks, history of mechanic searches.
  • Technical data: the device’s push notification token (platform, app version), the time of the last reading of conversations / channel, notification preferences, the connection diagnostic log (time, type of network error, app version — without message content; it is uploaded only after you sign in), the app error reports you choose to send from the “Something went wrong” screen (the error type and its technical details — the error message and stack trace, from which e-mail addresses, phone numbers and identifiers are removed automatically —, the screen, the platform, the app and operating system version and a short code derived from your account, used only to count how many users are affected; no e-mail, phone or message content; deleted after 30 days).
  • Local storage on the device / in the browser: authentication session (encrypted on the phone), preferences (language, theme, filters), messages not yet sent (local queue), photo cache. On the web we use only the storage strictly necessary for operation; we do not use tracking or advertising cookies, which is why we do not show a cookie banner. The web map loads Google Maps when you open the map screen (Google may set its own cookies; see Google’s policy).

3. Who sees what

  • Telephone number: NOT public. It is seen only by the user with whom you have an open conversation (after an accepted application or a conversation you started).
  • Registration number, chassis number and exact position of a breakdown announcement: seen only by you, the accepted mechanic and administrators. Other mechanics see the announcement with the position rounded to approximately 1 km and without the plate or chassis number.
  • Name, photo, company, specialisation, area, ratings: visible to signed-in users and, for mechanics, to visitors without an account (public service provider profile). The mechanics’ address and VAT number are shown in the public profile, as information of a service provider.
  • Visitors without an account (guests) can see: the list of mechanics (public profile), active Market listings, open breakdown announcements (without plate or chassis number, with approximate position, with the author’s name) and messages in the public community channel, but they cannot write, contact anyone or see contact data.
  • Private messages are visible only to their participants; channel messages are visible to all users and visitors.
  • Push notifications may contain the title and an excerpt of a message or listing; they pass through the services of Expo, Google (Android) and Apple (iOS) — see §5.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (account, announcements, applications, chat, Market, notifications): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Safety, moderation, prevention of abuse, rate limits, the connection diagnostic log, the error reports you send: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to keep a functional and safe service for professionals.
  • Legal obligations (DSA reports, requests from authorities, record of moderation actions): legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Optional notifications (new Market listings, mentions) and location: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Deleting the account: from the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or by e-mail. Details: the page “Delete account”.

7. Moderation, reports and administrative access (DSA)

  • You can report content or users from the app and you can block any user. Anyone (including without an account) can send a report to abuse@trucksos.net or through the form at https://trucksos.net/report. We confirm receipt and decide within 48 hours for manifestly illegal content, respectively 7 days otherwise; the author of the content receives a notice with the reasons for the decision (in the app, in the section “Moderation notices”, and by push) and can contest it within 14 days at the same address. Possible actions: removal of the content, temporary or permanent suspension of the account.
  • Administrators do NOT have general access to private conversations: they can only see a limited window of messages around a reported message or, in a report against a user, the latest messages between the reporter and the reported user. Each access is recorded in the administrative log. Administrator accounts use two-step authentication.

8. Your rights

Access, rectification (directly in the app), erasure (from the app), restriction, objection, portability (“Download my data” in Settings gives you a file with your account data and the content you created, including the reports you filed from the app, twice a day; photos and files appear as references, and messages are limited to the most recent 10,000 from private conversations and the most recent 10,000 from the channel; without the technical logs — the connection diagnostic log and the error reports), withdrawal of consent (the switches in Settings). We reply within 30 days at the latest to support@trucksos.net. You can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — in Romania, ANSPDCP (the Romanian supervisory authority, www.dataprotection.ro) — or with the authority in your country.

9. Who the service is for

TruckSOS is intended for professionals in road transport and service workshops. The service is reserved for persons aged at least 18; you confirm this when you sign up. We do not knowingly process data of minors; if we learn of such an account, we delete it.

10. Security

Encrypted connections (TLS), passwords managed by the authentication provider, session stored encrypted on the phone, access to data based on row-level security rules (RLS), the Apple token encrypted in the database, administrators with two-step authentication. No method is absolutely secure; if an incident affecting you occurs, we will inform you as required by law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. For important changes we will ask you to confirm it again the next time you open the app; the version and date are shown above. Previous versions are available on request.
