TruckSOS connects truck drivers whose vehicles have broken down with available mechanics. The application only facilitates contact: the price, the carrying out of the work and the payment are agreed directly between the driver and the mechanic. Price offers in applications are indicative and non-binding until the parties reach a direct agreement. TruckSOS is not a party to the contract between them, does not guarantee the availability, quality or price of the work, and does not charge any commission. The Market section allows users to publish sale listings for parts, tyres, trucks and trailers. Negotiation, payment and delivery take place directly between buyer and seller, outside the application; TruckSOS does not process payments, does not verify products and does not guarantee the transaction. The community channel is a public space for discussion between users; messages are visible to everyone, including visitors without an account.
